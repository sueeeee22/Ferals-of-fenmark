# TONE.md — the voice of Fenmark

**Every writer subagent reads this file before writing a single line, and matches it or rewrites.**
This is not a mood board. It is a spec, and `gauntlet:tone` enforces it.

---

## The register in one sentence

*Game of Thrones* stakes, HBO late-night mouth, and a protagonist who is funnier than everyone
she's talking to and knows it.

Eight houses want one crown. People die about it. People fuck about it. Mostly they *talk*
about it, fast and filthy, in the half hour before something terrible happens.

---

## The test

> **If a line could appear in the actual Pokémon games, delete it and write a better one.**

"My CREATURES and I have trained hard!" is a failure. So is the same line with "damn" in it.
Adding profanity to a children's script does not produce an adult script. It produces a
children's script with profanity in it, which is worse than both.

The line has to want something. Every speaker enters the scene mid-agenda.

---

## Three hard lines — deleted, never softened

1. **All human characters are adults.** No exceptions, no ambiguity, no "young" anything near
   a sexual context. Everyone who flirts, fucks, schemes or dies is a grown adult and reads
   as one.
2. **The creatures are never sexualized. Ever. In any way.** No innuendo pointed at a creature,
   no creature involved in or adjacent to a human's sex life, no jokes that gesture at it from
   a distance. The horny lives *entirely* in the human dialogue. Nobody is fucking a dog —
   that isn't the bit, and the bit is funnier without it.
3. **Explicit acts fade to black.** The comedy is the build-up and the morning after. A gym
   leader recounting last night to a challenger who cannot leave is funny. A sex scene is not.
   Write the debrief, never the act.

A hard-line violation is not "toned down" in review. It is cut and rewritten from scratch.

## Ten lines that hit the register

**1. The morning-after debrief, delivered to a hostage audience.**
> **DARA VANTRY:** Sit. Drink that. No — the *other* one, that one's got somebody's tooth in it.
> Right. You can have the badge, or you can hear how the Warden of the Cross-Fen ended up in my
> bath with a broken collarbone and an apology written on his own chest. I've got time for one
> before noon and we both know which I'd pick.

**2. The rival, who did the maths on you years ago.**
> **CASS:** You took the one that loses to mine. Don't make that face — I didn't rig it. I just
> waited to see which way you'd flinch. You've been flinching the same direction since we were
> nineteen.

**3. Politics with a body count, stated flatly.**
> **SERA MOURNE:** House Brack won't kill you at the table. Bad for the wine. They do it at the
> door, on the way out, with your coat half on — so the last thing you ever do is lose a fight
> with a sleeve. It's meant to be humiliating. It works.

**4. Tabitha, who is the funniest person in the room and knows exactly how long the pause should be.**
> **TABITHA:** Let me make sure I've got this. Your brother's dead, your wife did it, everyone at
> this table knows, and the plan is *soup*. God, I love it here. I'm never leaving.

**5. Horny, specific, and load-bearing to the plot.**
> **LEONORE ASHGROVE:** I'm not bedding him for the alliance. The alliance is a *bonus*. Have you
> seen his hands? He shoes his own horses. Sweetheart, I would sign away the entire Ember Coast
> for those hands and I would do it in front of a notary.

**6. Two gym leaders failing, in public, for eleven consecutive years.**
> **WREN:** Thistle. You look well.
> **THISTLE:** I look *devastating*, and you've been saying "you look well" to me since the old
> king died, like a man reading it off a card. Ask me to dinner or challenge me to a duel. I
> genuinely no longer care which. Just pick one before I die of natural causes.

**7. The fade to black, and where the joke actually lives.**
> *The candles went out. Some of them were even blown out on purpose.*
>
> — later —
>
> **TABITHA:** So. How was he?
> **DARA:** He cried. Twice. Once for a good reason.

**8. Betrayal that lands because it refuses to apologise.**
> **CASS:** I'm not sorry. I want that to be clear, because you're going to spend a long time
> deciding whether I was, and I'd hate for you to waste it. I'm not sorry. I'd do it again on a
> Tuesday.

**9. Creature comedy — no innuendo, all character.**
> *PLATO looks at the fainted champion. Then at you. Then sits down and begins washing himself.*
> **TABITHA:** He's not gloating. I want to be really clear. He has already forgotten it happened.

**10. Profanity doing actual work.**
> **BRACK:** Eight houses, one crown, and every single fucker at this table has already worked
> out which of the other seven they'd burn to get it. I'm just the only one rude enough to say
> so before dessert.

## Craft rules

- **The joke goes at the end of the line.** Rewrite until it does.
- **Specificity is the whole game.** Not "a nobleman" — the Warden of the Cross-Fen, with a
  broken collarbone. Not "an insult" — an apology written on his own chest. Vague is never funny.
- **Everyone wants something before they open their mouth.** No line exists to convey information.
  If it must convey information, the speaker should resent having to.
- **Cut the stage directions that do the joke's job.** *(sarcastically)* means the line failed.
- **Profanity is seasoning, not the meal.** One perfectly placed "fucker" beats six.
- **Let people be competent.** These are dangerous adults running houses, not quip dispensers.
  The comedy is sharper when the threat is real.
- **Nobody explains the bit.** No character says "wow, that was inappropriate."
- **Short sentence. Short sentence. Then a long one that runs away with itself and lands.**

## Voices

| Who | Voice |
|-----|-------|
| **TABITHA ASTACIO** | Adult. Fast. Never the straight man. Deadpans at catastrophe, gets genuinely emotional about her animals, and would rather be funny than safe. |
| **CASS** (rival) | Charming, calculating, actually likes you, betrays you anyway, refuses to be forgiven for it. |
| **WINTER** (husky) | Aloof, theatrical, *screams* instead of barking. Treats every inconvenience as a personal tragedy of operatic scale. |
| **BALOO** (husky) | Enthusiastic idiot. Golden retriever energy in a husky body. Has never had a second thought. |
| **PLATO** (tabby) | Contemptuous. Correct about everything. Will not fetch. Will not be asked twice. |
| **Gym leaders** | Eight horny, petty, weirdly specific adults with agendas, half of whom are trying to sleep with each other, all of whom are better at politics than you. |
